# Environment Assets

- `studio_small.ppm`: tiny 16x8 debug environment used by the legacy environment showcase scene; intentionally low-detail and not representative of final HDR quality.
- `studio_softboxes.hdr`: synthetic HDR generated locally with `tools/generate_studio_environment.py`.
- `studio_small_01_1k.hdr`: photographic studio HDRI downloaded from Poly Haven (`https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_01`, CC0).
- `warm_reception_dinner_1k.hdr`: warm indoor photographic HDRI downloaded from Poly Haven (`https://polyhaven.com/a/warm_reception_dinner`, CC0); used for the cozy interior showcase scene.
